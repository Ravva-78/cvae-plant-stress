How It Works

A Conditional Variational Autoencoder (CVAE) is trained on healthy and stressed plant images. The model learns to encode images into a compact latent space (dim=50) conditioned on the class label, then reconstruct them.

At inference, the reconstruction error (MSE between input and output) acts as an anomaly score. Healthy plants are reconstructed with low error. Stressed plants, having learned different latent patterns, produce higher error. A midpoint threshold separates the two classes.

Architecture
Input dim64 × 64 × 3 = 12,288
Hidden dim512
Latent dim50
ClassesHealthy, Stressed
LossBCE + KL Divergence
OptimizerAdam (lr = 1e-4)
